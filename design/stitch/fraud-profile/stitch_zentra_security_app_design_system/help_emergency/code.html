Immediate Action Required?

If you suspect your account has been compromised, lock it immediately to prevent unauthorized access.

What do you need help with?

Emergency Guidance

  1. Do not share your OTP or PIN with anyone, including individuals claiming to be Zentra support. We will never ask for these details.
  2. Review your recent transactions carefully. If you see unrecognized activity, report it immediately using the 'Payment Fraud' option above.
  3. Change your password if you suspect your account has been compromised or if you clicked on a suspicious link.
  4. Contact our 24/7 fraud hotline for urgent assistance if you cannot access your account or suspect ongoing unauthorized activity.
